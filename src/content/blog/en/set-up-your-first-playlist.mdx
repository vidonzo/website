Getting your channels into a player sounds fiddly and almost never is. If you
have your subscription details to hand, adding your first playlist in Vidonzo
takes about a minute. Here is the whole path, plus the couple of details that
quietly cause most first-time problems.

Before you start, decide which kind of source you have. Providers give out either
Xtream Codes logins or an M3U link, and the setup differs slightly. If you are not
sure which you have — or which is better —
[this short comparison sorts it out](/blog/xtream-codes-vs-m3u/).

## If you have Xtream Codes

1. Open Vidonzo and choose **Add playlist → Xtream Codes**.
2. Enter the **host** exactly as your provider wrote it, including `http://` or
   `https://` and the port number if there is one.
3. Enter your **username** and **password**. They are case-sensitive.
4. Give the playlist a name you will recognise, and save.

Vidonzo contacts the server once, pulls your categories, films, and series, and
builds the list. Your password is never stored in a form the app can read back.

## If you have an M3U link

1. Choose **Add playlist → M3U**.
2. Paste the full URL, ending in `.m3u` or `.m3u8`.
3. Name it and save.

If your provider gave you a file instead of a link, load it from your device
storage the same way.

## The details that trip people up

- **Copy, do not type.** A single wrong character in a host or password is the
  most common cause of a playlist that will not load. Paste from your provider's
  message.
- **Mind the port.** `http://example.com` and `http://example.com:8080` are
  different addresses. Include the port if you were given one.
- **One wrong `s`.** `http` and `https` are not interchangeable; use whichever
  your provider specified.

## After it loads

Once the list appears, mark a few favourites so your everyday channels sit at the
top. From there, [your phone can act as the remote](/blog/your-phone-is-a-better-remote/)
for the big screen, and if a channel ever buffers, it is almost always
[a network issue you can fix in a minute](/blog/fix-iptv-buffering/) rather than
anything wrong with your setup.
