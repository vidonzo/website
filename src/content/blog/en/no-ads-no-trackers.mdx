Vidonzo contains no advertising SDK, no analytics library, and no behavioural
tracking. Not a reduced amount. None.

That is easy to claim and easy to check: the app's dependency list is short and
public, and there is no network call to anything that is not your own provider,
your own guide URL, or — if you turn sync on — our sync service.

## Why it matters more here than elsewhere

An IPTV player sees an unusually intimate stream of data. What you watch, when
you watch it, how long you stay, what you rewind. In an ad-funded app that is
not a side effect; it is the product. Bundling an ad SDK into a player means
handing that behavioural stream to a third party you have never heard of, on
every device you install it on.

We would rather not have it.

## What we lose

Being honest about the trade: we do not know how many people use the app, which
features get touched, or where users give up. When something breaks, we find out
because someone emails us, not because a dashboard went red.

That is a genuine cost, and it makes the app slower to improve in places. We
think it is the right cost to pay for a category of app that sits this close to
what you watch at home.

## What that means for money

Vidonzo is free. There is no subscription, no paid tier gating playback
features, and the app processes no payments at all. The only money in this
picture is what you already pay your own IPTV provider — which has nothing to do
with us.

## What we do store

If you never sign in, nothing at all leaves your device. If you turn on sync,
we store your email, your encrypted playlist sources, your favourites, watch
progress and settings, and the devices you have linked. That list is exhaustive
and it is written out in full in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

Not "we may collect certain information to improve your experience". Just the
list.
