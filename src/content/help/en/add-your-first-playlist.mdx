Vidonzo ships with no channels. The first thing it asks for is a playlist you
already have. There are three ways to provide one.

## Xtream Codes

Choose **Xtream Codes** and enter the three values your provider gave you:

- **Host** — the server address, for example `http://example.com:8080`. Include
  the port if there is one.
- **Username**
- **Password**

Xtream is the richest option: live channels, films and series all arrive
together, along with the programme guide.

## An M3U or M3U8 link

Choose **M3U URL** and paste the address. Vidonzo fetches the playlist, caches
it, and re-reads it when it changes. If your provider also gives you an XMLTV
guide URL, add it in **Settings → Programme guide** so live channels show what
is on now.

## A local M3U file

Choose **Local file** and pick an `.m3u` or `.m3u8` file from your device. Nothing
leaves your device in this mode, and no internet connection is needed to browse
the list — only to play a stream that points at one.

## After it loads

The first load reads the whole catalogue and matches artwork, which can take a
minute on a large playlist. After that it is cached, and re-opening the app is
immediate.

You can keep several playlists side by side and switch between them from the
source picker at the top of the home screen.

## If it fails

- **Wrong credentials** — the most common cause. Retype the password rather than
  pasting it; trailing spaces are easy to copy by accident.
- **The host is missing its port** — many Xtream servers do not run on port 80.
- **Your network blocks the provider** — try again on mobile data, or set a proxy
  in **Settings → Network**.
