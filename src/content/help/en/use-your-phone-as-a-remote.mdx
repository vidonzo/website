Any device running Vidonzo can control the television running Vidonzo, over your
local network. Browsing a twenty-thousand-channel list with a d-pad is
unpleasant; doing it on the phone already in your hand is not.

## What you need

- Vidonzo installed on both the television and the controlling device
- Both devices on the **same local network**

## Pairing

1. On the TV, open **Settings → Remote control**. It shows a QR code and a short
   code.
2. On the phone, open **Remote**. Nearby televisions appear automatically —
   Vidonzo announces itself over mDNS, so there is nothing to type.
3. Pick the TV, or scan the QR code if you would rather be explicit.
4. Confirm on the television.

The pairing is remembered, so later sessions connect straight away.

## What you can do from the phone

- Browse and search the television's whole library
- Start playback with one tap
- Queue what plays next without interrupting what is on screen
- Add and remove favourites
- Use the trackpad for pointer control and the phone keyboard for typing

## From a smartwatch

A paired watch gives basic transport control — play, pause, skip and volume —
which is enough when the phone is in another room.

## If the TV is not discovered

- Confirm both devices are on the same network, not one on Wi-Fi and one on a
  guest network.
- Some routers enforce **client isolation** between wireless devices, which
  blocks the two from seeing each other; turn it off, or connect the TV over
  Ethernet.
- Scanning the QR code bypasses discovery entirely and is worth trying first.
