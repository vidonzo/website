Most playback failures are not the player. This is the order worth checking.

## Does another channel work?

Try two or three other channels from the same playlist.

- **Everything fails** — the problem is the playlist, the account or the network.
- **One channel fails** — that stream is offline or dead at the provider. Nothing
  in the app can fix it.

## Is the account still valid?

Xtream accounts expire, and many providers cap how many devices may stream at
once. If you are watching on the TV and the phone at the same time, you may
simply be over the limit. Sign out on one device and try again.

## Buffering rather than failing

Raise the cache in **Settings → Playback**. A larger cache absorbs an unstable
connection at the cost of a slower start. If the stream is 4K and the device is
old, try a lower-quality variant of the same channel where your provider offers
one.

## The stream drops mid-playback

Vidonzo reconnects automatically. If it keeps dropping, the provider is
throttling or the network is unstable — a wired connection on the TV usually
settles it.

## Nothing loads at all

- Check the playlist still loads: pull to refresh on the home screen.
- If your network blocks the provider, set a proxy in **Settings → Network**.
- Check the device clock. A wrong system time breaks TLS, and streams over HTTPS
  will fail with no obvious reason.

## Still stuck

Write to [support@vidonzo.com](mailto:support@vidonzo.com) with the device, the
Vidonzo version, whether the source is Xtream or M3U, and what you saw. Those
four things resolve most reports on the first reply.
