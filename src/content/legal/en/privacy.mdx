Vidonzo is a media player. It does not sell channels, does not host content, and
does not need to know what you watch in order to work. This policy describes the
data the app and the optional sync service handle.

## Using Vidonzo without an account

You can use Vidonzo without signing in. In that mode everything — your
playlists, favourites, watch progress, downloads and settings — is stored only on
your device. Nothing is sent to us.

The app contacts your own IPTV provider and any programme-guide URL you
configure. Those requests go directly from your device to that provider, and
what they log is governed by their policy, not ours.

## If you turn on sync

Signing in creates an account so your playlists and progress can follow you
between devices. We then store:

- **Account**: your email address, handled by our authentication provider, and an
  optional display name.
- **Playlist sources**: your Xtream host, username and password, and any M3U URL,
  stored **end-to-end encrypted**. They are encrypted on your device with
  AES‑256‑GCM before upload, under a key derived from your sync password with
  Argon2id. We hold only ciphertext and cannot read them. The label you give a
  playlist is stored in plain text, because it is your own display name for it.
- **Favourites, watch progress and app settings**, so they match across devices.
  These are stored in plain text and protected by per-user access rules.
- **Devices**: a name you choose, the device type, a public key, and when it was
  last seen — enough to link a new device and to revoke one you have lost.

Your sync password never leaves your device. Because of that, **we cannot
recover it**, and losing it means your encrypted playlists cannot be recovered
either.

## What we do not do

Vidonzo contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics or behavioural tracking.
We do not build a profile of you, do not sell or share personal data, and do not
process payments.

## Retention and deletion

Sync data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and
everything attached to it at any time — see
[Account and data deletion](/account-deletion/). Data stored only on your device
is removed by deleting it in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling
Vidonzo.

## Children

Vidonzo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note it in
the app. Questions go to [support@vidonzo.com](mailto:support@vidonzo.com).
