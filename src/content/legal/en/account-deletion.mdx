You can delete your Vidonzo account and all data attached to it at any time.

## Deleting your account

Email [support@vidonzo.com](mailto:support@vidonzo.com) from the address your
account uses, with the subject **Account deletion**. Writing from the account
address is how we confirm the request is yours.

We process deletion requests within **30 days**. When it completes, the
following is permanently removed:

- your account and profile
- your encrypted playlist sources
- favourites, watch progress and synced settings
- your linked devices and their keys

Deletion is final. Encrypted playlists cannot be restored afterwards, so export
or note anything you still need first.

## Data stored only on your device

Some data never reaches our servers and so is not covered by the request above.
Remove it yourself by:

- deleting individual playlists, downloads or history inside the app, or
- clearing Vidonzo's app data in your system settings, or
- uninstalling Vidonzo, which removes everything it stored locally.

## If you never signed in

If you have only ever used Vidonzo without an account, there is nothing on our
side to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Questions
